Does it work automatically?
Yes — just navigate to any supported sportsbook. Tab Lock detects it and locks that tab instantly. No buttons, no setup.
What happens if I navigate away from a sportsbook?
Tab Lock redirects the tab back to its assigned sportsbook. If you want to free a tab, just click "Clear" in the popup.
Is the basic version really free?
Yes, completely free — lock up to 4 sportsbooks with no time limit. Pro unlocks unlimited books, auto-refresh, and session restore.
How do I activate Pro after subscribing?
After payment you'll get a license key. Paste it into the Tab Lock extension popup under Settings → Pro License and click Activate.
Does it work with SmartStake?
Yes — SmartStake is fully supported and Tab Lock is optimized for SmartStake's one-click betting workflow.
Can I cancel Pro anytime?
Absolutely. Cancel through the Stripe customer portal at any time. Your Pro features stay active until the end of your billing period.
