Privacy Policy

Sportsbook Tab Lock Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: March 2026

Short version: Sportsbook Tab Lock does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything the extension does stays on your device.

1. What We Are

Sportsbook Tab Lock ("Tab Lock", "we", "our") is a Chrome browser extension that automatically assigns one sportsbook website to each browser tab. It is published by an independent developer and available on the Chrome Web Store.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Tab Lock does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identifiable information (name, email, address)
  • Browsing history or web history
  • Financial or payment information
  • Authentication credentials or passwords
  • Location data
  • User activity (clicks, keystrokes, mouse position)
  • Website content
  • Health information

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Tab Lock uses Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.sync APIs to store the following data only on your device:

  • Which browser tab is assigned to which sportsbook (tab IDs and domain names)
  • Your auto-refresh interval preferences
  • Your saved session layout (list of sportsbook URLs you chose to save)
  • Your Pro license key (if you have subscribed to Tab Lock Pro)

This data never leaves your browser except for the license key verification described below. It is not sent to any server, third party, or analytics service.

4. Pro License Verification

If you subscribe to Tab Lock Pro, the extension sends your license key to our license verification server (yourdomain.com/verify) to confirm your subscription is active. This request contains only your license key — no personal data, no browsing data, and no device identifiers.

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your credit card information. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

When you subscribe, we store your email address and Stripe customer ID on our server solely for the purpose of managing your subscription and delivering your license key. This data is never sold or shared with third parties.

5. Permissions Used and Why

  • tabs — To detect which sportsbook is open in each tab so the extension can assign and enforce the one-tab-per-book rule.
  • storage — To save your tab assignments, settings, and session data locally on your device.
  • webNavigation — To intercept navigation events in real-time and redirect tabs back to their assigned sportsbook if you navigate away.
  • alarms — To schedule automatic page refreshes at intervals you choose, so odds stay current.
  • host permissions (<all_urls>) — Required to monitor navigation across all sportsbook domains and apply tab locking behavior. The extension only acts on supported sportsbook URLs and ignores all other websites.

6. Third-Party Services

Tab Lock does not integrate with any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only third-party service used is Stripe, and only for Pro subscribers during payment and subscription management.

7. Children's Privacy

Tab Lock is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 18 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the extension after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
